# Red Stick Figure

Red is a red stick figure with no face. He walks around Minecraft, looks at you
when you come close, and has his name over his head. He's a friend, so players
can't hurt him.

Find the **Red Spawn Egg** in the Spawn Eggs tab.

Made by Elduin. Fabric, for Minecraft 1.21.11 and 26.2.
